Job Hunter Tracker

I've run projects for five years, but the worst-managed one was my own job search: no plan, no metrics, no retros. So I turned it into a real process.

Problem

Duplicate applications across DOU and LinkedIn, statuses overwritten in a spreadsheet so the history was lost, and 50+ career pages to check by hand.

What I built

A parser for company career pages with must/nice/stop-word filters, a tracker that keeps every stage change with dates, follow-up reminders, and a funnel dashboard.

PM lens

Track transitions, not current status. That turned "something isn't working" into a concrete answer about which stage was failing.

AI in the loop

Built with Kiro (Claude inside) as my dev partner. When a site's layout breaks the regular parser, the tool falls back to AI.

Hardware · Habit tracker Prototype in progress

Pix: a pocket pet that lives on your habits

A small creature on a round screen. Finish a goal and Pix eats and cheers. Skip for a few days and it goes to its hut, but it never dies. Gentle, not punishing.

The product

Up to 6 daily or weekly goals, a goal ring around Pix, streaks, sound and vibration. Works fully offline, and the goal library is edited on its own local web page.

Hardware

ESP32-S3, a round GC9A01 display, a real-time clock, a battery with sleep mode and my own carrier board. NFC check-ins are planned for v2.

How it's run

A PRD with P0/P1/P2 scope, a task board and a bug log. Two Claude Code agents: Builder writes firmware, Tester writes tests and files bugs. Only Tester can close a task.

My role

PM and "hands and eyes": I own scope and design, solder, measure current and answer the Tester's yes/no hardware checks with photos.
