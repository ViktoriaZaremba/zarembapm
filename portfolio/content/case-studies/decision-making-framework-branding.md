## Context

**Company:** Blackthorn.ai

I managed the development of the company's corporate website. Although technically it was a relatively simple project, it became one of the most challenging initiatives because the company's branding and visual identity had never been clearly defined.

Requirements changed frequently, different stakeholders had conflicting opinions, and the project was staffed opportunistically using whichever engineers happened to be available.

As a result, progress repeatedly stalled because the team could not reach alignment on fundamental branding decisions.

## Challenge

- Constantly changing requirements and stakeholder preferences.
- No agreed decision-making process for branding.
- Multiple previous attempts had failed to establish a consistent visual identity.
- Development repeatedly stopped because key branding decisions remained unresolved.

## My Role

- Proposed replacing endless discussions with a structured decision-making process.
- Coordinated external designers to prepare several branding concepts.
- Defined objective evaluation criteria for comparing design options.
- Identified key stakeholders whose opinions should influence the final decision.
- Designed a weighted voting framework that reflected each stakeholder's role in the decision-making process.
- Facilitated the evaluation process and aligned the CEO on the final decision.

## Result

- The company successfully established a unified visual identity.
- Website development was able to continue without further strategic uncertainty.
- The selected branding has remained the company's official visual identity to this day, including its logo, colors and overall brand style.
- The structured decision-making approach eliminated repeated cycles of subjective discussions.
