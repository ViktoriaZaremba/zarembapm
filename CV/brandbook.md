# Brand Rules — Structured Delivery Identity

## Brand Essence

The brand represents:
- structured execution
- scalable systems
- intelligent delivery
- operational clarity
- data-driven decision making

The visual language should communicate:
- precision
- confidence
- calm professionalism
- enterprise-grade reliability

Avoid emotional, chaotic, noisy, or overly “startup hype” aesthetics.

---

# Visual Identity

## Design Principles

- whitespace-first layouts
- minimalism over decoration
- structured compositions
- analytical visual language
- soft enterprise aesthetics
- subtle motion and detail
- hierarchy through typography, not colors

### Background Line Patterns
Meaning:
- depth
- context
- operational environment
- system presence

Purpose:
- decorative background layer behind content
- adds visual richness without competing with text
- creates sense of structured environment

Approved forms:
- concentric circles (offset from center, partial visibility)
- concentric arcs (not full circles — cropped by edges)
- parallel curved lines (topographic / contour style)
- S-curves and growth curves
- intersecting circle systems (Venn-like overlaps)

Style:
- stroke only (no fill)
- stroke-width: 1–1.5px
- colors: grid-gray, steel-blue, soft-gold (one per line or alternating)
- opacity: 0.2–0.35 (visible but never dominant)
- positioned asymmetrically (offset to corner or edge)
- may extend beyond visible area (cropped by container)
- spacing between lines: generous, not crowded

Pairing:
- combine with dot grid in opposite corner
- combine with vertical gold accent line
- combine with data points at intersections
- never combine with data curves in same area (choose one or the other)

Avoid:
- perfectly centered compositions
- too many concentric rings (max 4–5)
- equal spacing between all lines (vary the rhythm)
- high opacity that competes with content
- straight geometric grids in same space (use separately)
